# AI-assisted development notice

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

Workspace Widget was developed with assistance from generative-AI coding
tools. Human maintainers selected the requirements, reviewed and revised the
implementation, ran the release and security checks, and accepted
responsibility for the published source and release decisions.

The shipped application:

- does not contain a generative-AI model;
- does not call a generative-AI API or service;
- does not accept prompts; and
- does not generate user-facing text, images, audio, video, or other AI
  output.

This notice is a voluntary development-transparency statement. It is not a
claim that Workspace Widget is an AI product or service, and it is not legal
advice.

## Why this wording is used

As of the review date, Article 31(2) of Korea's AI Basic Act requires an AI
business operator providing generative AI, or a product or service using it,
to mark output generated by that generative AI. The Act defines generative AI
as a system that generates content and defines an AI user business operator
as one that uses another provider's AI to provide an AI product or service.
Workspace Widget's released functionality does neither.

AI assistance used while writing ordinary software does not, by itself, make
the distributed launcher a generative-AI product or make the launcher produce
generative-AI output. If a future version adds AI generation or an AI-backed
service, maintainers must reassess notice, transparency, privacy, copyright,
and security duties before release.

The Korean Copyright Commission also publishes current AI copyright guides.
Maintainers should retain meaningful human review and verify the rights and
licenses of code, media, fonts, icons, and other material before accepting a
contribution or release.
